    Who went out?

      Finishing the round

      Each team commits when its counting is done — they don't have to finish together. The round closes on its own once both have.

      Read from a photo

      One photo of one team's books, taken from above with the whole hand in frame. The reader finds every card's corner index; you check it, fix anything it got wrong, and add the counts to the round.

      Reading

      Turn it upright, then trim

      Turn the photo until the rank letters in the card corners are the right way up. This one matters: the reader cannot see a sideways glyph at all, and a photo left on its side finds nothing rather than finding it badly. Then drag the four yellow bars in to cut away everything around the cards. Trimming the sides matters as much as the top: a stray card lying past the last book gets read, and being read it lands in some book or other.

      Check the cards

      Pinch to zoom, drag to move the photo. The crosshair stays still in the middle — line it up on a card's corner index and use the buttons below. Take out lifts whatever the crosshair is sitting on.
